# Approved External Entity Descriptions

Prepared: 8 August 2026
Status: copy-ready; account-owner action required for third-party profiles

## Short

Envi Tech AL provides environmental laboratory testing, field monitoring and
consultancy support in Pakistan. Its permanent laboratories hold location-specific
PNAC accreditation: Karachi LAB-285 and Lahore LAB-347. Only work listed in the
applicable current scope may be described as accredited.

## Medium

Envi Tech AL provides environmental testing, water and wastewater analysis, field
monitoring, calibration coordination and environmental consultancy support from
Karachi and Lahore. The Karachi permanent laboratory holds PNAC LAB-285 and the
Lahore permanent laboratory holds PNAC LAB-347 under ISO/IEC 17025:2017. Each
accreditation is limited to the matrices, parameters and methods in that branch's
current published PNAC scope; service availability alone does not establish
accredited status.

## Long

Envi Tech AL supports organizations in Pakistan with environmental laboratory
testing, water and wastewater analysis, field monitoring, compliance evidence and
technical consultancy. Its permanent laboratory premises have separate,
location-specific ISO/IEC 17025:2017 accreditations from the Pakistan National
Accreditation Council: Karachi LAB-285 and Lahore LAB-347. Accreditation applies
only to the matrices, parameters, methods and other limits in the applicable
current PNAC scope. Other regulatory and management-system credentials are
separate categories and should be checked against their current issuer records.
Customers should share the location, sample matrix, parameter list, method and
report purpose so the applicable capability and credential status can be confirmed
before work begins.

## Mandatory external corrections

- Never attribute LAB-284 to Envi Tech AL.
- Replace generic “PNAC/ISO 17025 accredited laboratory” wording with the applicable
  branch and current-scope qualification.
- Do not claim that reports are universally accepted by SEPA, EPA, buyers,
  certification bodies or courts. Acceptance depends on the authority, purpose,
  sampling, method, scope and current credential.
- Do not claim “Green Lab Gold”, “200+ clients”, outcome percentages, legal standing
  or client names unless a current evidence record and publication permission exist.
- Historical LinkedIn/social posts containing broad accreditation, regulatory
  acceptance or legal-standing claims require owner-side correction or removal.

## Lahore reputation controls

Use only a genuine claimed location, accurate NAP data, real local photographs and
authentic customer feedback. Ask all eligible customers neutrally; never buy
reviews, use staff reviews, gate dissatisfied customers, duplicate the location or
keyword-stuff the business name. Respond factually and never disclose client work,
sample results or confidential relationships.
