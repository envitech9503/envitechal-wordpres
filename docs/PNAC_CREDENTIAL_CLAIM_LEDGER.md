## Prohibited claims and extrapolations

- LAB-284 must never be attributed to Envi Tech AL.
- Neither branch scope may be attributed to the other branch.
- Organization-level accreditation wording must name both locations or clearly
  point to branch-specific scopes.
- A service, matrix, parameter, method, range, sampling activity, field activity,
  calibration, or report must not be called accredited merely because Envi Tech AL
  has a PNAC credential.
- LAB-285 does not establish accredited calibration, air, stack, noise, soil,
  microbiology, ballast-water, or consultancy scope.
- LAB-347 does not establish accredited calibration, air, stack, noise, soil,
  microbiology, metals, COD, fluoride, or consultancy scope.
- Capability and accreditation are separate. A published service may be available
  without being within either accredited scope.
- Validity dates must not be stated without the qualification that current PNAC
  status and surveillance control.
