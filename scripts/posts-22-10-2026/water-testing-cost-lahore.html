Key takeaways

  • The cost of a water test is the sum of its parameters, each with its own method and effort. The parameter list, not the headline, is what you are buying.
  • Microbiology, metals by atomic absorption and organic parameters cost more than basic chemistry, because they take more instrument time, consumables and quality control. They are also the parameters that answer health questions.
  • Sampling, collection and courier logistics are priced separately from analysis, and for regulatory work they are not optional.
  • A written scope from the laboratory (parameters, methods, accreditation status, sampling, reporting date) is the only basis on which two quotations can be compared.
  • Samples from Rawalpindi, Islamabad, Faisalabad, Gujranwala, Sialkot and Multan reach Lahore by overnight courier for chemistry, or by field visit for microbiology.

What a water test quotation is made of

A quotation from any serious laboratory is a list of parameters with a method beside each one, plus the services around the analysis. Understanding the pieces is the quickest route to a sensible price.

Why two quotations for "the same test" differ

Put two quotations side by side and the gap almost always comes from one of five places. The parameter count: one laboratory's drinking water panel has nine parameters, another's has twenty-two. The method: arsenic by a screening kit and arsenic by atomic absorption are different tests with different detection limits and different standing with the Punjab EPA. Accreditation: results within a PNAC scope cost more to produce, because every batch runs with quality control, and some laboratories quote non-accredited results without saying so. Sampling: included, extra, or left to you. Reporting: a bare result sheet, or a report with the method, the limit and a verification route. A quotation that is 30 percent cheaper because it drops microbiology or uses a field kit for metals is a different product, not a discount.

Scoping the test to the decision

The cheapest good test is the one that answers your question and nothing else. The patterns below cover most requests we receive in Lahore and from the rest of Punjab.

  • Household borewell or tube well. Basic chemistry, nitrate, fluoride, arsenic, iron and microbiology. Arsenic and fluoride are the two Punjab groundwater parameters that most often exceed PEQS without any change in taste.
  • Tanker or filtration plant supply. Microbiology and TDS on each new supplier; a monthly sample for hotels, hospitals and schools.
  • RO plant check. TDS and microbiology at the outlet, feed TDS for comparison; every three months or after servicing.
  • Housing society or school supply. Full PEQS drinking water panel at commissioning, then a reduced panel at the frequency the society or authority sets.
  • Factory effluent for Punjab EPA. The parameters named in the EMP or approval, read against PEQS effluent limits, sampled by the laboratory's field team with chain of custody.
  • Export buyer or brand audit. The parameters in the buyer's specification, within PNAC scope, with the accreditation mark on the report.

If you are not sure which pattern fits, send the water source, where it is, what the report is for and the deadline. The laboratory's reply should be a written scope you can compare with any other quotation line by line.

How to brief the laboratory

  1. Water source: tap, borewell, tanker, RO outlet, effluent, process water.
  2. Location, and whether you can bring the sample to Johar Town, want collection, or will courier it.
  3. Purpose of the report: household, society, hotel or hospital compliance, Punjab EPA submission, buyer audit, bank.
  4. Any standard or specification you must meet (PEQS, WHO, NEQS, a buyer document).
  5. Deadline, so the laboratory can give a reporting date.

With those five answers the laboratory can confirm the parameter list, the methods, which results fall within its PNAC scope (LAB-347 for the Lahore laboratory) and the reporting date, and give you a quotation that means something. The contact page has the phone, WhatsApp and email routes.

Frequently asked questions

How much does water testing cost in Lahore?

It depends on the parameter panel, the methods, whether results must be within an accredited scope, whether the laboratory samples on site, and the reporting purpose. A basic household panel and a full PEQS drinking water panel with metals and microbiology are different products at different prices. Ask for a written scope and compare quotations parameter by parameter.

Is there a water testing laboratory price list for Lahore?

Laboratories price per parameter and per service, so a meaningful quotation is built from your source, purpose and parameter list rather than read from a flat list. Envi Tech AL provides a written scope and quotation on request, usually the same working day.

Which parameters should a Lahore borewell test include?

pH, TDS, hardness, chloride, sulphate, nitrate, fluoride, arsenic, iron, and total coliform with E. coli. Arsenic and fluoride are the groundwater parameters in Punjab most likely to exceed PEQS without affecting taste.

Can I send a sample from Rawalpindi or Islamabad?

Yes. Chemistry and metals travel by overnight courier in the bottles the laboratory supplies. Microbiology must arrive within 24 hours of sampling, which an overnight courier usually meets; otherwise a field visit is arranged.

Does the price include sampling?

Sampling and collection are quoted as their own line, so you can see what you are paying for. For Punjab EPA submissions and audits, sampling by the laboratory's team with chain of custody is the right choice; for a household check, self-sampling in the laboratory's bottles is acceptable.

Will the report be accepted by Punjab EPA?

Envi Tech AL's Lahore laboratory is listed as a certified environmental laboratory by the Punjab EPD and holds PNAC accreditation LAB-347. Reports state the method, limit and accreditation status for each parameter, which is what the agency reviews. Acceptance of a submission depends on the results and the rest of the file.

How long does a water test take in Lahore?

It depends on the panel. Microbiology needs incubation time and some metals run in batches. The laboratory gives a reporting date at booking.
