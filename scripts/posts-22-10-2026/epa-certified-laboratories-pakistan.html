When a Sindh EPA or Punjab EPA submission is rejected for its laboratory data, the reason is rarely the numbers. It is that the laboratory was not on the agency's list, or was approved for different parameters, or its certificate had lapsed. "EPA certified laboratory" is a specific legal status in Pakistan, separate from PNAC accreditation, granted province by province, for a fixed term, and sometimes for some tests only. This guide explains what the status means in Sindh and in Punjab, where each agency publishes its list, how to read an entry, and how it fits with ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation. Envi Tech AL holds both forms of recognition at its environmental testing lab in Karachi and its environmental testing lab in Lahore, and we use our own entries as the worked example.

Key takeaways

  • EPA certification is provincial. Sindh EPA certifies laboratories under the Sindh Environmental Quality Standards (Certification of Environmental Laboratories) Regulations 2014; Punjab EPD issues Certificates of Approval and publishes the list on its website. Neither recognises the other's certificate automatically.
  • Certification is for a fixed term and must be renewed. In Sindh the certificate runs for two years; Punjab certificates carry their own validity dates, Envi Tech AL's current Lahore certificate running 2025 to 2028.
  • Certification can be partial. The Sindh regulations allow the agency to approve a laboratory "for certain purposes or parameters only", so the parameter list matters as much as the name.
  • PNAC accreditation (ISO/IEC 17025) is a different thing: an assessment of technical competence by the national accreditation body. For regulatory monitoring you normally need the EPA certificate; for buyers, banks and courts you usually need the accreditation; a serious laboratory holds both.
  • Verify both before you rely on a report: the EPA list for the province, and the PNAC directory for the scope.

What "EPA certified" means in Sindh

The Sindh Environmental Protection Agency certifies laboratories under the Sindh Environmental Quality Standards (Certification of Environmental Laboratories) Regulations 2014, made under section 37 of the Sindh Environmental Protection Act 2014 and notified in the Sindh Government Gazette on 19 December 2014. The regulations define an environmental laboratory as one certified by the agency, and give it a defined set of functions: to test samples of air, water, soil, effluent and waste sent by any factory or person authorised by the agency and determine whether they comply with SEQS; to measure noise from industrial activity or vehicles; to issue test reports; to carry out studies the agency requires; and to send the agency an annual report listing every test report issued.

To be certified, a laboratory must meet the criteria in regulation 5: premises in a clean area away from sources that could contaminate its work, a building suitable for a laboratory, qualified and experienced scientific staff and the equipment listed in the regulations' schedule, a scientific system for recording and reporting results, proper waste disposal, and payment of the scrutiny and certification fees. A committee of the agency's officers examines the application, which can include sending trial samples, obtaining sample test reports and an on-site assessment visit. The agency may reject the application, accept it in full, or accept it partially for certain purposes or parameters only. A certificate is issued in the prescribed form and the laboratory is notified in the official Gazette.

Two details matter when you rely on a Sindh certificate. First, validity: a certificate is valid for two years from its date unless suspended or revoked earlier, and remains valid while a renewal application is pending. Second, conditions: a certified laboratory must follow the methods the agency stipulates, keep calibration records for its equipment, display its certificate and fee list, and submit its annual report. The agency may suspend or revoke certification after giving the laboratory a hearing, and a revoked laboratory cannot reapply for at least a year. Envi Tech AL's Karachi laboratory in Bahadurabad holds Sindh EPA certification alongside PNAC accreditation LAB-285.

What "EPA certified" means in Punjab

In Punjab the Environment Protection Department (EPD), which houses the Punjab Environmental Protection Agency, approves environmental laboratories and publishes the result on a single page of its website, "EPA Certified Laboratories", under its Resource Centre. At the time of writing the page lists 21 laboratories. Each entry gives the laboratory's name and address and links to its Certificate of Approval as a PDF; some entries carry a second certificate, for example an EPD approval for vehicular emission testing. The page also links to the conditions attached to private laboratory certificates, a document on implementing those conditions, and the schedule of laboratory test rates.

The Punjab page does not print validity dates as text, so the certificate itself is the document to open. Envi Tech AL's entry at 87-E Madina Heights, Johar Town, Lahore links to a Certificate of Approval covering 2025 to 2028, alongside PNAC accreditation LAB-347 for the same site. For any Punjab EPA submission, the laboratory producing the monitoring data should appear on this list with a certificate in date.

Certification and accreditation are not the same thing

A laboratory can hold one without the other. An EPA certified laboratory with no PNAC accreditation can produce data the agency accepts, but an export buyer's auditor may not accept its results for the parameters in a specification. A PNAC accredited laboratory with no EPA certificate can produce technically excellent results that the provincial agency will not take for an EMR. For a facility that answers to both a regulator and a buyer, which describes most textile, food and pharmaceutical exporters, the laboratory needs both, at the site that does the work. How to check a laboratory before you rely on it

  1. Identify the province. A facility in Karachi, Hyderabad or Sukkur answers to Sindh EPA and SEQS; one in Lahore, Faisalabad or Sialkot answers to Punjab EPA and PEQS. The laboratory needs the certificate for the province whose agency will read the report.
  2. Find the laboratory on the agency's list. For Punjab, the EPD's certified laboratories page, with the certificate PDF. For Sindh, the agency's published list and the Gazette notification; ask the laboratory for a copy of its certificate if the list is not current.
  3. Read the certificate. Check the name and address match the laboratory that will actually test your sample, the validity dates, and whether the approval is limited to certain parameters.
  4. Check accreditation separately. Confirm the parameters you need are in the laboratory's PNAC scope at that site.
  5. Look at the report format. It should cite the method, the limit from SEQS or PEQS beside each result, and the certificate and accreditation numbers, and it should be verifiable. Envi Tech AL reports are checked on the report verification portal.

Why the distinction protects you

The provincial agencies built certification around reporting discipline: stipulated methods, calibration records, an annual list of every report issued. That is what lets an inspector trust an EMR without re-sampling. PNAC built accreditation around technical competence, test by test. When a facility uses a laboratory that holds both, each reader of the report finds the assurance they were looking for. When it uses a laboratory that holds neither, or the wrong one, the cost usually appears months later as a rejected submission or a failed audit, long after the sample is gone. Frequently asked questions

What is an EPA certified laboratory in Pakistan?

A laboratory certified by a provincial Environmental Protection Agency to test samples and produce monitoring data for that agency's purposes. In Sindh the certificate is issued under the Sindh Environmental Quality Standards (Certification of Environmental Laboratories) Regulations 2014; in Punjab the Environment Protection Department issues a Certificate of Approval and lists certified laboratories on its website.

Where is the list of EPA certified labs?

Punjab publishes it on the EPD website under Resource Centre, EPA Certified Laboratories, with each certificate as a PDF. Sindh EPA notifies certified laboratories in the official Gazette and publishes its list through the agency; ask the laboratory for its certificate if you cannot find the current list.

How long is Sindh EPA laboratory certification valid?

Two years from the date of the certificate, unless suspended or revoked earlier. The certificate stays valid while a renewal application is being decided.

Is a PNAC accredited lab automatically EPA certified?

No. They are separate recognitions from separate bodies. A laboratory applies for each one, and a regulatory submission normally needs the EPA certificate for the relevant province.

Can a laboratory be certified for some parameters only?

Yes. The Sindh regulations allow the agency to accept an application "partially for certain purposes or parameters only", so check the certificate's scope, not just the laboratory's name on the list.

Which certificates does Envi Tech AL hold?

The Karachi laboratory holds Sindh EPA certification and PNAC accreditation LAB-285. The Lahore laboratory holds a Punjab EPD Certificate of Approval (2025 to 2028) and PNAC accreditation LAB-347. Each applies to its own site and published scope, and copies are on the accreditations page.

Does a Sindh certificate work for a facility in Punjab?

Not for Punjab EPA submissions. Certification is provincial, which is why a laboratory serving both provinces needs a certificate from each agency.
