Searches for a water testing lab in Pakistan usually come from one of two places: a city with no accredited laboratory of its own, or a site that is a long drive from the nearest one. The practical question is the same in both cases. Can a sample taken in Hyderabad, Multan, Faisalabad, Quetta or Peshawar reach an accredited laboratory in good enough condition for the result to mean anything? It can, for most parameters, provided the sample is bottled, preserved and dispatched correctly. This guide explains how Envi Tech AL receives samples from across Pakistan at its water testing lab in Karachi and its water testing lab in Lahore, which parameters travel well, which do not, and what to do about the ones that do not.

Key takeaways

  • Envi Tech AL operates two permanent laboratories, in Bahadurabad, Karachi (PNAC LAB-285) and Johar Town, Lahore (PNAC LAB-347). Samples from any city in Pakistan can be tested at either one.
  • Most chemical parameters (TDS, hardness, chloride, sulphate, nitrate, fluoride, arsenic and other metals) tolerate 24 to 48 hours in transit when cooled.
  • Microbiology (total coliform, E. coli) must be analysed within 24 hours of sampling. From a distant city this means a morning courier, ice packs and a sterile bottle, or a field team visit.
  • pH, temperature and residual chlorine cannot be posted at all. They are measured at the tap, either by the client with a calibrated meter or by our sampling team.
  • Call before dispatch. The laboratory confirms the parameters, sends the right bottles and preservatives, and books the sample in, so the courier is not wasted.

The pattern is clear. Chemistry travels. Microbiology travels only with a fast courier. Field parameters do not travel. A sensible plan for a distant site therefore measures pH, temperature and chlorine on the spot, sends chemistry by two-day courier, and either books a same-day courier or a field visit for microbiology.

How to send a sample from another city

The steps below are the ones we ask clients to follow. Skipping any one of them is the usual reason a sample is rejected or a result is flagged as outside its holding time.

  1. Call or message the laboratory first. Tell us the city, the water source (tap, borewell, tanker, RO outlet, effluent), what the report is for (household, Sindh EPA or Punjab EPA submission, export buyer, bank or audit), and the date you plan to sample. We confirm the parameter list, quote, and send bottles and preservatives by courier if needed. Contact details are on the contact page.
  2. Use the bottles supplied, or the right substitute. Clean plastic for general chemistry, acid-preserved plastic for metals, a sterile bottle for microbiology. A rinsed mineral-water bottle is acceptable for TDS and hardness only; it is never acceptable for microbiology or metals.
  3. Sample in the morning. Run the tap for two minutes, fill the chemistry bottles to the shoulder, and fill the microbiology bottle without touching the inside of the cap or neck. Leave an air gap in the microbiology bottle. Label each bottle with the source, date and time.
  4. Measure the field parameters at the tap. pH and temperature at minimum. If you have no meter, say so on the form and we will note that pH was measured at receipt rather than in the field.
  5. Cool and pack. Place the bottles in an insulated box with frozen ice packs, not loose ice and never in a freezer. Seal the box.
  6. Fill in the chain-of-custody form. Who sampled, where, when, what preservation was used, and the courier consignment number. Photograph it before dispatch. The form is what turns a bottle of water into evidence.
  7. Dispatch the same morning by overnight courier. Send the consignment number to the laboratory so the sample is booked in before it arrives and logged within minutes of receipt.

When a field visit is better than a courier

Some work should not be done by post. If the report is going to a regulator, if microbiology is the question, if several points on a site must be sampled on the same day, or if the sampling itself needs to be defensible, our field team samples on site. The team carries calibrated meters, preserved bottles, cool boxes and chain-of-custody forms, and the sampling record becomes part of the report. Field sampling is routine across Karachi, Hyderabad and the Sindh industrial belt from the Karachi laboratory, and across Lahore, Faisalabad, Sheikhupura, Kasur and Gujranwala from the Lahore laboratory. For cities further away we group visits, so ask about the next scheduled trip to your area.

Regulatory sampling deserves a particular mention. Sindh EPA and Punjab EPA expect effluent and drinking water samples behind an Environmental Monitoring Report to come from a certified laboratory, and the sampling record is read as closely as the results. What the report compares your water against

A result on its own is a number. The report becomes useful when each parameter is read against the limit that applies to you. For drinking water in Pakistan that is the Pakistan Environmental Quality Standards for drinking water (PEQS) and, where a client asks for it, the WHO Guidelines for Drinking-water Quality. For effluent it is the Sindh Environmental Quality Standards (SEQS) in Sindh, the Punjab Environmental Quality Standards in Punjab, and the National Environmental Quality Standards (NEQS) elsewhere. The report states which standard was used for each parameter, so a reader in Quetta and a reader in Karachi reach the same conclusion from the same page. Accreditation and what it covers

Both laboratories are accredited by the Pakistan National Accreditation Council to ISO/IEC 17025. Accreditation is scope-bound: it applies to the specific matrices, parameters and methods listed in each laboratory's current scope, not to water testing in general. A parameter outside the scope is still tested and reported, but the report marks it as such. If your buyer or regulator requires accredited results for a particular parameter, tell us at the quotation stage and we will confirm whether it is within scope at the laboratory you are sending to. Frequently asked questions

Is there a water testing lab near me if I am outside Karachi and Lahore?

Envi Tech AL's laboratories are in Karachi and Lahore. From any other city in Pakistan you can send chemistry samples by overnight courier after a call to confirm bottles and parameters, or book a field visit for microbiology and regulatory sampling.

Which city's laboratory should I send my sample to?

Whichever your courier reaches faster. As a rule, Sindh and Balochistan send to Karachi; Punjab, Islamabad, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Azad Kashmir send to Lahore. Both laboratories issue the same report format.

Can bacteriological water testing be done by courier?

Only when the sample reaches the laboratory within 24 hours of sampling, in a sterile bottle, cooled with ice packs. A same-day or overnight courier from a nearby city works. From a distant city, a field visit is the reliable option.

Can I use a mineral water bottle for the sample?

For TDS, hardness, chloride and similar general chemistry, a rinsed mineral water bottle is acceptable. For microbiology or metals it is not; those need a sterile bottle and an acid-preserved bottle respectively, which the laboratory supplies.

How much water is needed?

One litre covers a standard drinking water chemistry panel. Microbiology needs a separate 250 mL sterile bottle. Metals need a separate 250 mL preserved bottle. For a full PEQS panel plan on about two litres in three bottles.

How long does a result take once the sample arrives?

It depends on the parameter list. Microbiology needs incubation time, and some metals are run in batches. The laboratory gives a reporting date at booking, which is the date to plan around.

Will the report be accepted by Sindh EPA or Punjab EPA?

Envi Tech AL's Karachi laboratory is Sindh EPA certified and the Lahore laboratory is listed by Punjab EPA. Reports state the method, the standard applied and the accreditation number for each parameter, which is what the agencies review. Whether a submission is approved depends on the results and the rest of the file, not on the laboratory.

How do I check the report is genuine?

Every report carries verification details that can be checked on the report verification portal using the report number and date.
